## Findings of the comparison
1. **E1, E2, E7, E8, E9, E10 are corollaries or restatements of known results** (FSC-POMDP identification, Causal Hierarchy Theorem/IV, Pinsker-type sensitivity, Kruskal latent-class identification, PRG/PRF theory).
2. **E5/Theorem 3** is the only statement for which no corollary relation was found in the accessed sources; its K = 1, m = 2 case is a corollary of Hellman–Cover (original not accessed).
3. **Not resolved by this comparison:** whether Wolpert's inference-device limits and a finite-state embedded observer with *host-determined* setup yield any distinct theorem; the general-m finite-memory question under hidden structure; the literature on finite-memory *active* testing.
