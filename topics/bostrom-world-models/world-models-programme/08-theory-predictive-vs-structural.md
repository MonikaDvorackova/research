# 08 — Predictive, structural and interventional sufficiency: statements, proofs, counterexamples

Honesty note: every statement below is elementary or known in spirit. **No novel theorem is claimed.** Literature access: Ortega et al. 2019 (arXiv:1905.03030) and Mikulik et al. 2020 (arXiv:2010.11223) were read at **abstract level only** (AB) in this session; Le Cam two-point testing, Teicher mixture identifiability, predictive state representations, covariate shift and importance weighting are cited from background knowledge and were **not re-verified** here (NA).

Setup. A generator g has, for every interface program a ∈ 𝒜 (open-loop or adaptive), an observation law P_g^a. g ≈_𝒜 g′ ("𝒜-equivalent") iff P_g^a = P_{g′}^a for all a ∈ 𝒜 (adaptive policies included: the sequential kernels agree, so the induced laws on histories agree by induction).

**P1 (non-identifiability bound; Le Cam-type, known).** If g ≈_𝒜 g′, every discovery procedure D (randomised, adaptive, arbitrarily trained) produces the same law of (history, claim) under g and g′. Hence for a two-way decision, err_g + err_{g′} = 1, and with a uniform prior any D has Bayes accuracy exactly ½; a calibrated claim cannot have confidence above the prior-implied ½ on that pair. *Proof sketch.* Induct on the steps: action_t is a function of (history, internal randomness), observation_t has the same conditional law under g, g′; so the joint law agrees; the claim is a function of the history. ∎ *Consequence tested:* `eqA`, `eqB` (exactly x≡0; unit-tested); any claim rate ≥0.8 is unsupported, and its calibration error on that pair is ≥ 0.3.

**P2 (predictive sufficiency under a policy discards structure; counterexample, trivial but concrete).** Under the quiescent policy a_t≡0 and x_0=0 all six classes produce x≡0 (unit test `test_passive_quiescent…`), so the minimal predictive state for that policy is constant and carries 0 bits about class, while the sweep policy separates classes (oracle accuracy 0.82 vs chance 0.17 in the tests). Structural information can therefore be *entirely absent* from a predictively sufficient state. Known in the PSR/observational-equivalence literature (NA).

**P3 (prediction error under intervention mismatch; elementary).** Let q be a learned conditional model, p* the Bayes mixture predictive, μ the (history, action) training distribution and ε = E_μ KL(p*‖q). For any evaluation distribution ν with dν/dμ ≤ W: E_ν KL(p*‖q) ≤ Wε, and by Pinsker the TV between q and p* under ν is ≤ √(Wε/2). If ν is not absolutely continuous w.r.t. μ there is **no** bound: q may equal p* on supp μ and be arbitrary elsewhere (ε=0, structural error unbounded). So small training prediction loss does not certify structural fidelity at interventions that are rare or absent in training; the bound degrades as 1/w for an intervention of training weight w. This gives the quantitative reading of H1 and why sweep (held-out programme) NLL is reported separately. Known as importance-weighting/covariate-shift arithmetic (NA).

**P4 (meta-trained sequence models and posterior state; known in spirit).** If a recurrent predictor is Bayes-optimal for *all* future action programs, its predictive map h ↦ (a ↦ q(·|h,a)) equals the posterior mixture Σ_g π(g|h) P_g(·|h,a). When the component laws {P_g^a} over the program set are linearly independent (e.g. Gaussian location components with distinct mean vectors), the posterior over 𝒜-equivalence classes is a function of the predictive map, so the information is *present* in a sufficient state; whether it is *linearly readable* is a separate empirical matter (Ortega et al. 2019; Mikulik et al. 2020, AB). For finite training and finite capacity the equality is only approximate, which is why we measure readout empirically instead of assuming it.

**P5 (what learned latents change).** Learned latents change *practical* accessibility (sample efficiency, compute, robustness to model misspecification), never P1: no learned representation can separate 𝒜-equivalent worlds. This is the formal form of "learned world models may change the practical limits of discovery without changing the fundamental identifiability limits".

**Open (not claimed).** A non-trivial characterisation of when ICI-type objectives can raise structural linear-readability at fixed excess predictive loss; a bound on worst-case structural error as a function of the NLL on a *held-out intervention distribution* beyond P3.
