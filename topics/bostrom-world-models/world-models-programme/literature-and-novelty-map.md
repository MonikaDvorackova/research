## 2. What is already established (answers to the brief's examples)
* **Observationally equivalent mechanisms cannot be distinguished using those observations** — definitional; behavioural equivalence/realization theory (system-dynamics matrix; PSR) states exactly the identifiable content.
* **Restricted interventions preserve equivalence; richer interventions refine it** — Hauser–Bühlmann; Causal Hierarchy Theorem; Prop 1′/P2.
* **Computational indistinguishability can prevent efficient discrimination of information-theoretically distinguishable objects** — PRF/PRG theory (Aaronson §7.1), Kearns–Valiant, Berthet–Rigollet (conditional).
* **Predictive accuracy does not give structural identification** — non-identifiability and misspecification; Locatello Thm 1; underdetermination (SEP).
* **Embedded-inference impossibility results independent of physics** — Wolpert 2008.
None of these may be presented as a contribution of this programme.
