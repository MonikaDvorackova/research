## 1. What was built
* **Environment (SDW).** Hidden x2; observed x1 (actuator-filtered u1), x3, nuisance x4; two admissible interventions (u1 on the actuator, u2 injecting into x2); noise 0.03 + process noise; per-instance random linear constants; truth integrated with 4 substeps. Nine mechanism families: lin, sat, delay (*known*); quad, gate, backlash, fb = hidden cubic self-damping (*unfamiliar, in-language*); osc = hidden resonant second-order mode (*outside the language*); unreach = threshold above the actuator reach (*interventionally equivalent to no coupling*, tested exactly). Zero input is identical for all families (tested).
* **Hypothesis language.** ≤ 2 coupling primitives from {linear, quad, cube, tanh(s), threshold(θ), dead-zone(θ), delay(k), play(w)} plus an optional hidden self-cubic-damping term; every hypothesis is executable (predicts x3 for any admissible programme, tested on unseen programmes). Fitting: skeleton (a, b3, k) from the u2-only episode, forward structure search by BIC, joint least-squares polish, a second pass with the skeleton re-estimated. **Caveat that governs every result below: the primitives are the vocabulary of the simulator's constructions** (threshold ↔ gate, play ↔ backlash, delay ↔ delay, hidden damping ↔ fb); only osc is outside. This is *structure selection in an expressive but generator-aligned grammar*, not open-ended hypothesis generation.
* **Model criticism.** Per-episode χ² of residuals against σ̂ (estimated from actuator innovations; instrumental-variable AR fit), Bonferroni α = 0.01; out-of-sample χ² on the latest designed episode; ambiguity check (rival hypotheses within ΔBIC < 10 and max predicted RMSE disagreement over 124 admissible programmes > 0.5 σ̂ ⇒ abstain-ambiguous). Outcomes: claim / abstain-out-of-language / abstain-ambiguous.
* **Design.** Falsification-driven: maximise the standardised disagreement between the leading hypothesis and the three strongest structurally different alternatives over a fixed pool of 24 admissible programmes. Alternatives: fixed sweep A (u1 first), fixed sweep B (interleaved u1/u2), random, generic ensemble-variance (bootstrap NARX ensemble, E-SINDy/Plan2Explore-style), declared-class posterior-variance EIG, and the falsification design without any u2 programme.
* **Baselines (same observations, same budget, same shared first two episodes).** SINDYc via `pysindy 2.1.0` with four state sets (raw observed; derivative-embedding; learned estimator state; **oracle hidden state, privileged**) in two libraries (tuned degree-1, and a per-variable nonlinear dictionary); ridge NARX; Hammerstein static-map + linear chain (variable projection); declared-class Bayes grid (lin, sat, delay) with goodness-of-fit; the agent's own grammar search on a fixed sweep. Tuned on known families only (ns 24200). **Not run:** PySR (Julia unavailable), AI Feynman, weak-SINDy, implicit-SINDy (declared gap, `18`).
* **Learned representation.** Bidirectional GRU estimator (x3 noisy, u2 → hidden state), 3 seeds × 1500 steps × 6000 simulated known-family episodes (76 s each, CPU), simulator x2 labels used *only in training* (privileged-training disclosure); never trained on quad/gate/backlash/fb/osc/unreach.
* **Role A (neural imagination) not re-run here** (different environment); `17` stands as its test (negative).

### Pre-fixed rules
* **H1 (structure generation beats generic identification): SUPPORTED against the listed baselines, with a strong caveat.** Agent minus best listed baseline (Hammerstein) on the unfamiliar in-language families: **+0.49 [0.41,0.57] at B=5, +0.51 [0.42,0.59] at B=8**; minus SINDYc-oracle-state +0.66 / +0.75; minus declared-class Bayes +0.62 / +0.70. Caveat: (i) the grammar's primitives are the generator's vocabulary; (ii) SINDYc/NARX/Hammerstein are *my configurations* (degree-1 SINDYc chosen by known-family tuning; nonlinear libraries diverged in free-run rollout); PySR, weak- and implicit-SINDy were not run; (iii) the script initially let the agent's own no-design variant count as a baseline; corrected before the verdict was read (`19` addendum B).
* **H2 (falsification design helps recovery): NOT SUPPORTED.** Against the better fixed sweep in all in-language families: B=3 **+0.07 [0.03,0.11]**, B=5 **−0.05 [−0.09,−0.02]**, B=8 **−0.02 [−0.04,−0.00]** (needs CI > 0 at ≥ 2 budgets). Equal to random and to the declared-class EIG design at every budget (CIs include 0); better than generic ensemble-variance design (+0.22 / +0.14 / +0.06). Median RMSE does not separate the designs (0.022 / 0.008 / 0.005 vs 0.018–0.030 for the others). Family-level trade-off at B=5: gate 0.62 (design) vs 0.80 (sweeps), backlash 0.85 vs 0.42.
* **H3 (criticism and abstention): failed as pre-stated, fixed by calibration, benefit unproven.** osc claim rate with criticism 0.03 (B=8), unreach recognised 0.90, AUROC of the misfit statistic for osc vs in-language truth 0.87 / 0.94 / 0.98 (B=3/5/8) — **but in-language false rejection 0.26 (full) and 0.15 (in-sample only) at B=8, 0.47–0.68 at B=3** against the pre-stated ≤ 0.10 ⇒ the pre-stated H3 **fails**. The declared-class Bayes goodness-of-fit flag does *not* flag osc here (AUROC 0.47–0.68; contrast `17`, 0.80–1.00, a different environment). **Follow-up** (`19` addendum B; 60 valid known-family fits at ns 27000 → threshold; fresh ns 28000, 270 instances): calibrated false rejection **0.03 [0.01,0.07] on the unfamiliar families at B=8 (0.03 known)**, osc claim rate 0.10, unreach 1.00, coverage 0.90, claim precision 0.97, decision accuracy 0.89 vs 0.86 for no criticism (paired difference +0.030 [−0.019,+0.078], i.e. not distinguishable), and +0.070 [0.041,0.104] over the uncalibrated rule. The pre-fixed "calibration artefact" rule is **MET** — the failure was miscalibration of the plug-in χ² test, and an empirical null estimated on known families transferred to unfamiliar valid structures. For the fixed-sweep-B agent, however, calibrated accuracy is *below* no criticism (−0.067 [−0.130,−0.004]): criticism trades osc false claims against false abstentions with no robust net benefit.
* **H4 (learned representation): supported against a naive analytic reconstruction, with the interpretation unresolved.** Proposal recall@3 (equation-error regression on the reconstructed hidden state, in-language families): oracle 1.00, **learned estimator 1.00**, analytic (Savitzky–Golay derivative + b3·x3) 0.87 [0.83,0.91]; learned − analytic **+0.13 [0.09,0.17]** (unfamiliar +0.13 [0.08,0.19]). The estimator trained only on known families transfers to unfamiliar structure and equals the privileged oracle — consistent with S5's premise (the x3→x2 inversion is mechanism-independent) but **contrary to its prediction that no learned advantage would appear**, because the analytic comparator is weak. A post-hoc Kalman/RTS-smoother reconstruction (one setting, q = 4, untuned) was *worse* (0.77), so neither analytic comparator is a strong model-based state estimator; a tuned smoother is untested. SINDYc rollout recovery with the learned state (0.39 in-language at B=8) equals the oracle state (0.38) and exceeds embed (+0.23 [0.18,0.28]) and raw (+0.10 [0.06,0.14]): **state reconstruction is not the SINDYc bottleneck — the library/rollout is** (the oracle-state dictionary SINDYc recovers only 0.06).
* **H5 (synergy): not supported.** Full agent decision accuracy (uncalibrated criticism) vs no criticism: −0.47 / −0.34 / −0.13 [−0.19,−0.07] (B=3/5/8); vs declared-class + EIG +0.25 [0.19,0.31] at B=8 (structure generation matters), vs generic ensemble-variance design +0.18 [0.12,0.24], vs random +0.11 [0.05,0.17], vs declared-class EIG design +0.12 [0.07,0.19]; vs fixed sweeps −0.22/−0.10/+0.04 (CI includes 0 at B=8); removing u2 interventions changes accuracy by −0.09 / −0.05 / +0.01. No single configuration beats all ablations at all budgets.

### Exploratory (not pre-registered) — the one design effect that replicated
With calibrated criticism (ns 28000, same instances, paired), falsification design vs fixed sweep B in decision accuracy: B=3 −0.026 [−0.067,+0.011], **B=5 +0.122 [+0.059,+0.185], B=8 +0.096 [+0.044,+0.148]**. Mechanism: *residual structural ambiguity* among in-language hypotheses within ΔBIC < 10 at B=8 is **0.10 (ns 25000) and 0.07 (ns 28000) with design vs 0.28 and 0.21 with sweep B**; at B=3 design is worse (0.70 vs 0.61 and 0.69 vs 0.62). Designed experiments resolve competing structures (more claims, equal precision) without improving predictive recovery, because near-equivalent structures predict equally well. Seen in two development sets, one design family, one fixed sweep; not a confirmatory finding.

## 3. Ablations in one place (decision accuracy, B=8, all 9 families, uncalibrated; ns 25000)
full 0.74 · no criticism 0.87 · in-sample criticism only 0.82 · sweep B 0.70 · sweep A 0.71 · random design 0.63 · ensemble-variance design 0.56 · declared-class EIG design 0.61 · declared class only 0.49 · no u2 in pool 0.73. Structure generation (declared-class-only 0.49 → 0.74) is the only component with a large, unambiguous effect; active design shows a small ambiguity-reduction effect; criticism *as pre-specified* costs accuracy through false rejection; calibrated, it is roughly neutral.

## 4. Failures and negative findings (kept)
1. Plug-in χ² criticism was miscalibrated (false rejection 0.15–0.62); first implementation also suffered from a hypothesis-simulator integration mismatch and a measurement-noise-biased AR fit (both fixed before evaluation, `19` A).
2. Falsification design did not improve functional recovery over a fixed sweep or random programmes; the ensemble-variance design (E-SINDy/Plan2Explore-style) was clearly worse — but it rests on a weak NARX ensemble, so this does not test ensemble-variance design with a good structured model.
3. SINDYc in my configuration is weak everywhere (≤ 0.68 in known families, ≤ 0.21 unfamiliar even with the oracle state); degree ≥ 2 polynomial libraries diverged in rollout. This limits H1's reach to the baselines as configured.
4. The osc family is *functionally* adequately fitted (recovery 0.78 at B=8) by in-language approximants; "abstain" is the right answer only under the strict normative definition of the protocol, not under predictive tolerance τ.
5. Declared-class Bayes goodness-of-fit was uninformative about osc (AUROC ≈ 0.5), reversing its role in `17`.
6. Term identity was substantially below functional recovery for fb (0.2–0.4): equivalent-by-prediction explanations are frequently chosen.
7. Gate recovery with the falsification design (0.62 at B=5) was worse than with sweeps (0.80).
8. The "analytic" reconstruction comparators for the representation role are weak (naive derivative; an untuned Kalman smoother that was even worse), so the learned-estimator advantage is not shown against a strong model-based estimator.
9. Role A (neural imagination) not re-tested; the first hypothesis of the mission (learned latent + structure search + design beats SR/system ID alone) is **not established**: structure generation gives the large effect, the learned estimator equals the oracle but its necessity is untested against a strong analytic estimator, and design shows only an exploratory ambiguity effect.

## 5. Statistics and compute
Instances are the replication unit; paired comparisons use identical instances, observation noise and shared episodes; bootstrap over instances only (3 estimator seeds were averaged into one state estimate, so seed variance is not in the CIs). Tolerance τ = 0.05, ΔBIC window 10, ε = 0.5 σ̂, α = 0.01 fixed before runs; ε sensitivity (0.25–2σ̂) changes coverage 0.55–0.73 and accuracy 0.62–0.76 at B=8 (analysis only). Compute: local CPU only; estimator 3 × 76 s; tuning ≈ 6 min; primary run 2074 s wall on 11 workers (~6 CPU-hours); calibration 81 s; test 350 s; no GPU/MPS, no paid resources.
