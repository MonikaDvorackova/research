# 17 — Active discovery of unfamiliar mechanisms with learned world models: results, ablations, mathematics, decision

Design and rules: `16` (written before any run). Code: `src/eid/mdw/`, `scripts/mdw_*`, tests `tests/test_mdw.py`. Raw: `results/mdw_dev/`. Namespaces: training 21000+, SysID-λ selection 22000 (known families only), smoke 22100, wm-diagnostic 22200, **evaluation 23000 (first look) and 23500 (fresh instance set)**; instances are the replication unit (n=120 per set: 40 per known family ×3, 120 gate, 120 backlash); world-model ensembles of 3 training seeds; supervised regressor 2 seeds. ## 2. Pre-stated rules
* **R1 (learned planning helps): not met.** v1: planner worse than sweep on gate (3.33 vs 2.62) and backlash (1.59 vs 1.28) and worse than random on both; v2: planner lower than sweep and random on backlash at B=4,8 (1.17/1.37 vs 1.82/1.92 and 1.49/5.31; non-overlapping CIs) but worse on gate and *worse than the no-information prior* (1.05) — a better-than-alternatives data policy for a model that is worse than not estimating. Planning (Plan2Explore-style ensemble disagreement) sends the agent to programmes where a poorly-generalising ensemble disagrees, not to informative ones.
* **R2 (world model needed): not met.** On held-out families the best world-model variant is worse than direct probing, SysID, and (on backlash) declared-class Bayes at B=2 and 4 (CIs far apart).
* **R3 (knows when out of family): met only by the declared-class Bayes goodness-of-fit flag** (AUROC ≥0.80, ≥0.99 for the sweep schedule); world-model spread is unreliable.
* **R4 (world model unnecessary): applies.** In this problem class the world model adds no measurable value over: direct probing (no model), generic SysID on the same data, or a structured model with a declared class (in-family).

## 3. Ablations (what each component contributes)
* Learned latent representation: context vs no context — in-family 0.71 → 0.58 (helps), out-of-family worse (gate 2.06 → 2.62; backlash 1.19 → 1.28): **negative transfer** of the meta-learned prior outside its family.
* Intervention planner: removing it (sweep) improves held-out results in v1 and v2/gate; helps only on v2/backlash.
* Structural hypothesis module (declared class): in-family best-or-equal (0.13); out-of-family worst (2.5–2.7 on gate) **unless its misfit flag is used** (then it abstains correctly).
* Uncertainty module: ensemble spread not reliable; Bayes GOF reliable.
* Representation vs imagination: the supervised head on the *same encoder* reaches 0.06–0.09 in-family while imagination-based descriptors reach 0.58 — the world-model's context state contains the information; the autoregressive imagination route loses it (free-run x3 RMSE after context falls only for the large probe, 0.28 → 0.12; small/neg probes barely improve, `wm_imagination_diagnostic.txt`).

## 4. Mathematics (all elementary/known; **no novelty claimed**)
* **M1 [elementary, unit-tested].** Every family is identical under zero input (x3 ≡ 0): passive data carry no structural information; interventions are necessary.
* **M2 [elementary, unit-tested]. Interface-limited exact equivalence.** x1 is a stable first-order filter of u1, |x1| ≤ (G/B1)·max|u1|; if (G/B1)·max|u1| ≤ θ the gated coupling never activates, so every threshold θ above the reach yields x3 ≡ 0 — exactly equivalent to each other and to "no coupling". θ is identifiable only through inputs reaching it, and then **only through the transient** (the end-state is independent of θ to ~5·10⁻⁴; measured). Known flavour of actuator-reach arguments.
* **M3 [elementary].** Any finite set of constant-amplitude probes yields steady-state information about the memoryless coupling at finitely many amplitudes; two couplings agreeing at those amplitudes have identical steady-state descriptors. Structural identification of a nonparametric coupling needs persistently exciting inputs (cf. Wagenmaker–Jamieson FT for linear systems); our four descriptors are a projection, which is why they can be measured directly.
* **M4 [elementary, bias–variance].** A direct measurement of a descriptor defined by a probe programme is unbiased for any mechanism; a model-based estimate has bias from the model class (declared class or pretrained prior), which dominates out of family. Hence a structured estimator wins when its class contains the truth or when the target is directly measurable, and pretrained/learned priors can only help when the family is near (consistent with Dasgupta et al.'s "in or near the training distribution", FT).
* **What active experimentation changes here:** statistical distinguishability (the identifying programme depends on the pair of mechanisms: amplitude for saturation, timing for delay, sign reversal for gating, cycling for memory), computational accessibility (closed-form for a declared pair), sample complexity (≥ one episode per independent descriptor for direct probing), and structural identifiability (reach limits, M2). These are kept distinct: M1/M2 are identifiability statements; B-dependence is sample complexity; the world model's failure is representational/computational.

## 5. Adversarial controls and honest caveats
* *Does a simple method solve it?* Yes: direct probing (when the targets are the probe responses) and SysID on the sweep.
* **Design caveat (important):** the descriptors are defined by the same four probe programmes the direct baseline runs, so direct probing is nearly exact by construction. The unseen-programme prediction test (§1) removes that alignment; it too does not favour the world model.
* *Structured baseline:* generous (knows all linear constants; only family, coupling gain and one parameter unknown).
* *Privileged information:* none in agents (tests check no `fam/par/label` inputs); descriptors/oracle used only for scoring. Observation noise paired across methods; schedules nested in B.
* *Leakage:* episode length, action range and resets identical across families; zero-input response identical (tested).
* *World-model quality:* 3 seeds, one architecture (GRU); two training configurations (v1, v2); one diagnostic (imagination RMSE). A stronger model (transformer, TD-MPC2-style, much larger training) was not tried; I do not claim world models cannot work here — only that these did not, at matched small compute, and that simpler methods did.
* *Statistics:* instance-level CIs; no CIs for the unseen-prediction numbers; the planner/ensemble effects are not separated from training-seed variance beyond the 3-member ensemble.

## 6. Bostrom
The environment makes concrete a point central to Bostrom's detectability question: mechanisms differing only beyond the interface's reach (M2) are exactly indistinguishable, and distinguishing the rest requires *interventions of particular kinds* (large amplitude, sign reversal, cycling, timing). Discovering hidden structure here (nonlinear saturation, delay, gating, hysteresis) corresponds to level 3 of the epistemic ladder (mechanism); it says nothing about implementation, simulation, or ancestor simulations. The intervention interface available to the toy agent (arbitrary programmes on two actuators with resets between episodes) does not exist for observers in our universe, who cannot reset the world, choose arbitrary controls of fundamental fields, or replay episodes — so even the successful direct-probing strategy has no direct analogue; the learned model's role would be to compensate for missing interventions, and here it does not.

## 7. Compute
CPU only (M4 Pro). World models v1: 3 × ≈10.5 min (5 concurrent jobs); v2: 3 × ≈49 min (H=192, 12,000 steps); regressors 2 × ≈8.5 min; each evaluation ≈ 80–85 s; SysID λ selection and diagnostics ≈ 1–2 min. No MPS/GPU, no paid resources.

## 8. Decision: **NO-GO** for the proposed direction; REVISE of the programme
*Novelty:* claim D is known for closed families (Dasgupta et al., FT) and learned-world-model exploration (Plan2Explore, FT); E (out-of-family) was **not** found in the sources read, and my experiment's answer is negative. *Advantage:* none found for a learned world model or learned planning over direct probing, generic SysID or a declared-class Bayes design with a goodness-of-fit check. *Therefore* the claim "learned world models give a genuine scientific advantage for active discovery of unfamiliar mechanisms" is not supported in this problem class (R4) and I recommend not pursuing it further at this scale. The stronger alternative identified: a **structured experimenter** (declared-class Bayesian design) **plus a misfit/goodness-of-fit detector** for out-of-family mechanisms (AUROC ≥ 0.80), with direct probing/SysID as the fallback estimator — i.e. the open scientific problem is *hypothesis generation after misfit* (what to do when the declared class fails), not representation learning. That problem (symbolic/structural model search triggered by misfit; AI-scientist and symbolic-regression literature, NA here) is untested.

## 9. Roadmap (evidence-gated)
* **M1 (read first, no compute):** AI-scientist/symbolic-regression/SINDy/program-synthesis literature (the untested claim E threat), Blackwell/Le Cam, Gohr, Yao/Blum–Micali; gate: stop if hypothesis-generation-after-misfit with held-out mechanism families is already demonstrated.
* **M2:** misfit-triggered hypothesis generation in MDW: when the declared-class GOF fires, search a compositional library (coupling = {linear, saturating, delayed, gated, backlash, compositions}) by interventions chosen to separate the top candidates; measure recovery of held-out constructions and false-claim rate; baselines: SINDy-like library regression, direct probing, world-model-guided proposal (does a learned model help *propose* the right library element? — the only place a latent model might still matter).
* **M3:** richer hidden mechanisms (spatial/multi-variable, adaptive precision/resource-limited generators) and intervention budgets with costs; test whether direct probing's advantage persists when the targets are not aligned with a fixed probe set.
* **M4:** an independent confirmatory design only if M2 shows a result that is not predicted by the prior art (preregistered; new namespaces ≥ 30000; reserved 600–629/700–719/800–829 untouched).
