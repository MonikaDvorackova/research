# Notes

## Purpose

Working research knowledge for this topic: claim inventories, analyses, and structured thinking that is not yet normative.

## What belongs here

- Claim inventories (claim → evidence → status)
- Analytical write-ups and working models
- Assumptions explicitly labeled as such
- Cross-links to `literature/`, `architecture/`, and `specifications/`

## What does not belong here

- Unfiltered brain-dumps better suited to `ideas/` or `inbox/`
- Camera-ready articles or Radar drafts (`publications/`)
- Normative specification text (`specifications/`)
- Full paper summaries (short pointers OK; full notes go in `literature/`)

## Naming conventions

- `<short-slug>.md` in kebab-case
- Prefer stable names for living documents (e.g. `claim-inventory.md`)
- Frontmatter `type: note`; keep `updated` current

## Design note

Notes are the main workspace for thinking. Prefer several focused notes over one ever-growing file so reviews stay tractable.
