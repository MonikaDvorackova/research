# Reconstruction Approach Comparison Matrix

## Purpose

This document evaluates existing approaches against the reconstruction
requirements defined in the reconstruction checklist.

## Evaluation Scope

The comparison focuses on whether each approach preserves sufficient
information to reconstruct:

1. the system state
2. the decision process
3. the evidentiary basis
4. the applicable rules and constraints
5. the human and organisational context
6. the resulting decision and its consequences
