# Review Methodology

## Objective

Evaluate existing approaches according to their ability to preserve
decision knowledge required for faithful reconstruction.

## Evaluation Unit

One publication, framework, standard, or engineering approach.

## Evaluation Procedure

1. Read the primary source.
2. Identify the information preserved.
3. Map preserved information to the reconstruction checklist.
4. Assign scores.
5. Record strengths.
6. Record limitations.
7. Record missing reconstruction capabilities.

## Scoring

0 = Not addressed

1 = Mentioned or partially supported

2 = Substantially supported

3 = Explicitly and systematically supported

## Evidence

Every score must be justified by explicit evidence from the source.

No assumptions are allowed.
