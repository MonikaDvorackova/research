## Becoming/submitting as an author

**VERIFIED.** `oreilly.com/work-with-us` states: *"Get in touch if you'd
like to write a book (or something shorter), create a video, develop a
tutorial — or tell us about any other interesting project you've been
pondering."* Contact route: **workwithus@oreilly.com**. The page
explicitly covers content shorter than a book, which an O'Reilly Radar
or Radar-adjacent article would fall under, though the page does not
use the word "article" or "Radar" specifically.

## Pitching article/content ideas

**VERIFIED (general route), NOT PUBLICLY SPECIFIED (article-specific

**VERIFIED that Radar is active** — `oreilly.com/radar/` is live,

## Proposal/contact route

**VERIFIED.** Two authoritative contact points found by direct fetch:

- **workwithus@oreilly.com** — general content/authorship proposals
  (`oreilly.com/work-with-us`), explicitly including "something shorter"
  than a book.
- **app.oreilly.com/authors/** — the "Authors" page, which states *"We're
  always on the lookout for new talent and ideas. Contact us or submit a
  proposal,"* then redirects back to the same "work with us" route
  rather than providing a separate process.

A third address, **booktech@oreilly.com**, was found but is explicitly
scoped to "technical questions concerning [existing] book content," not
new-content pitching — **not relevant to this submission.**
