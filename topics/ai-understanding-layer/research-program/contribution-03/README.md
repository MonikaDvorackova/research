## Contribution 3 — Historical/Comparative Literature Review

**Status: historical/comparative review complete. Article 3 NOT drafted.
No experiment run.** This directory executes `../research-roadmap.md`
Step 6 — the largest unaddressed research gap in the programme, flagged
there and in `../claim-graph.md` (node **C9m**) since 2026-08-21 as "not
yet audited at all."

This review's purpose, stated by the authorizing task and taken
seriously here: **try to destroy the terminal thesis** ("AI may be the
first major computing paradigm in which capability is scaling faster
than the mechanisms required to preserve knowledge about system
behavior") against established literature, not to confirm it.

## Reading order

1. `pre-research-claim-ledger.md` — the terminal thesis decomposed into
   five separable claims (A–E) plus a sixth fragmentation claim (F),
   written *before* any external search.
2. `historical-literature-review.md` — tests claim B (the recurring
   software-engineering preservation pattern) against the actual history
   of version control, transaction logs, and distributed tracing.
3. `program-comprehension-review.md` — tests claim A (AI systems are
   hard to understand) against the formalized program-comprehension and
   architectural-knowledge-management literatures.
4. `observability-review.md` — tests whether "understanding" reduces to
   control-theoretic/software observability.
5. `provenance-review.md` — tests whether provenance theory and
   deterministic replay already close the gap.
6. `ai-systems-review.md` — tests claims C and E (AI-specific difference,
   comparative "losing the race" claim) against ML-systems, foundation-
   model/agent, XAI, formal/runtime-verification, and assurance-case
   literature.
7. `prior-art-collision-matrix.md` — the consolidated comparison across
   every mechanism reviewed.
8. `source-ledger.md` — full citations for every substantive source used.
9. `earned-premises.md` — exactly what Contributions 1 and 2 justify as
   premises here, no more.
10. `residual-gap-analysis.md` — what, if anything, survives all of the
    above.
11. `novelty-verdict.md` — the novelty classification and the disposition
    of the term "Understanding Layer."
12. `research-questions.md` — the minimum open questions before Article 3
    could be drafted.
13. `VERDICT.md` — the final answer.

## What this review does not do

Does not draft Article 3. Does not design or run an experiment. Does not
modify Contributions 1 or 2, Source A/B, or any O'Reilly material. Does
not treat Source A's rhetoric as established fact anywhere in this
directory — every claim is reconstructed in neutral language before
being tested (`pre-research-claim-ledger.md`).
