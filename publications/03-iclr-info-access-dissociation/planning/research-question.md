# Research question — STOPPED

**Status:** `stopped — novelty OCCUPIED`

## Frozen question (historical; do not pursue)

When decision-relevant information is held fixed and presentation length is matched, does sequential tool-mediated acquisition reduce language-model agent task success relative to simultaneous presentation of the same information?

## Why stopped

The controlled simultaneous-versus-sequential identical-information comparison is already the central experimental object of Laban et al., *LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation* (ICLR 2026 Best Paper), including CONCAT and RECAP/SNOWBALL controls. Tool wrapping is not a material scientific residual.

## Do not reopen as

- “Lost in conversation for agents”
- “Sharded tool observations”
- “Information-access regime dissociation” without a *new* controlled factor beyond Laban

See `novelty-audit.md`.
