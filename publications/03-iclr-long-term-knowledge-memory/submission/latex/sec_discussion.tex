\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Alternative explanations}
\label{sec:alternatives}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Retrieval only:} Acc-tied B2/B3-$k3$; shortcuts Acc$>$0 with BCR$=$0.
\item \textbf{Conjunction only:} BCR lies below $\Acc^{n}$ for B4/H0 (residual beyond i.i.d.).
\item \textbf{Abstention only:} abstention predicts many $\Phi{=}0$ cases but Acc still overstates joint success on partial hits.
\item \textbf{Parser only:} Qwen BCR$=$0 stable across three parsers at low Acc; the proprietary-reader check reaches Acc~$=$~45.6\% with BCR~$=$~0/22 under the same ceiling/parser protocol, so the gap is not explained by a Qwen-only floor.
\item \textbf{Length/tier only:} frozen gaps persist across tiers; NL transfer uses short narratives as a control probe.
\item \textbf{Consistency$\neq$factual correctness:} \texttt{consistent\_wrong\_world}; narrative heuristic Acc$<$1 with BCR$=$1.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}
\textbf{Synthetic ontology} limits open-world claims; naturalistic NL transfer remains a heuristic control probe.
\textbf{Reader coverage:} frozen Qwen2.5-3B H4 plus one proprietary-reader transfer check (GPT-4.1~mini on 22 bundles). GPT-4.1~mini is proprietary but non-frontier; only one such reader was evaluated; six outputs were truncated at the predetermined ceiling. This is a system-level check (retrieval $+$ prompt $+$ model $+$ parser $+$ scorer), not model-only causation.
\textbf{Seed diversity} for B3 mini-sweeps is limited.
\textbf{Metric scope:} BCR is history-constraint compatibility, not usefulness/safety.
External human validation is absent.
