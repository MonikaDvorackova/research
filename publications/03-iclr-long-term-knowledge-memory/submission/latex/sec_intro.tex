\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Long-term memory systems must answer what is true now, what was true earlier, how transitions occurred, and which evidence supports those claims. Standard evaluations score these questions \emph{one at a time}~\citep{wu2025longMemEval,wu2026longMemEvalV2,hu2025memoryAgentBench,tao2026memConflict,xie2026dynamicMem,wang2026evoMemBench,hu2026everMemBench,liu2026worldMemArena}. Acc can therefore look high while dependent answers fail as one reconstructed world history (Figure~\ref{fig:inconsistency}).

\paragraph{Central claim.} Under known gold evolving histories, item-level Acc systematically overstates whether dependent answers jointly satisfy world-history constraints. We operationalize the missing object with $\Phi$ / BCR and $\Gapq=\Acc-\BCR$, and diagnose conjunctive strictness with an $\Acc^{n}$ baseline (not a generative model).

\paragraph{Approach.} \textbf{WorldConsistMem} supplies dependent bundles over one gold history, compiles machine-checkable constraints $\mathcal{C}$, and scores $\Phi=\mathbf{1}[\forall c\in\mathcal{C}:c(\hat Y)]$ on the predicted answer set. Acc is local gold match; BCR is the mean of $\Phi$. $\Phi$ is not ``exact-match conjunction'' alone: constraints can fail on incompatible transitions/provenance even when many items are locally correct, and can hold for coherent-but-wrong worlds.

\paragraph{Primary + confirmatory evidence.} Frozen Option~A (102/1{,}088/7{,}140) is the primary Acc--BCR divergence case. Separately labelled confirmatory probes include parser re-scoring of frozen Qwen H4, a 36-scenario NL control set, and a proprietary-reader transfer check on a frozen stratified H0 subset (149 queries / 22 complete bundles) with GPT-4.1~mini (Section~\ref{sec:prop-transfer}).

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item A formal evaluation object for dependency-linked belief consistency ($\Acc$/$\Phi$/BCR/$\Gapq$) with an explicit conjunction diagnostic.
\item WorldConsistMem: controlled evolving-world benchmark with machine-checkable history-derived constraints.
\item Empirical Acc--$\Phi$ divergence on the frozen symbolic study, including identical Acc with different BCR.
\item Confirmatory probes: parser re-scoring, bounded NL controls, and an independent proprietary-reader transfer check on complete bundles with conservative handling of truncated outputs---not open-world or frontier-model coverage.
\end{enumerate}
