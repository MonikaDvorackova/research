\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{LTM Acc suites} grade per-query competence under long histories~\citep{wu2025longMemEval,wu2026longMemEvalV2,hu2025memoryAgentBench,hu2026everMemBench,wang2026evoMemBench}. \textbf{State tracking / evolving knowledge} recovers values at times or checkpoints~\citep{xie2026dynamicMem,liu2026worldMemArena}. \textbf{Conflict / temporal} work shows correctness can diverge from retrieval fitness~\citep{tao2026memConflict}. \textbf{Logical cross-query consistency} already separates Acc from global coherence~\citep{salla2026crossQueryContradictions,chaudhry2026logicVault}; we do not claim that separation is new in general. Our residual is the evaluation \emph{object}: persistent memory over evolving multi-entity gold worlds with history-derived validity/transition/provenance/relation constraints scored jointly. Appendix~\ref{app:related} (Table~\ref{tab:closest}) details closest benchmarks.
