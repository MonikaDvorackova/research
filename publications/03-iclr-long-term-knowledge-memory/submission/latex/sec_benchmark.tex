\paragraph{Why synthetic?} Causal Acc-vs-$\Phi$ scoring needs a known gold history and auto-derived constraints. Open dialogue lacks that joint gold without a second noisy annotator. We isolate structural claims (Acc$\neq$BCR) under controlled seed/tier/$k$/corruptions; ecological validity is not claimed. A confirmatory naturalistic set (Section~\ref{sec:results}) tests bounded NL surface transfer only.
