\section{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}

We report metrics jointly. With $\Phi$ as in Section~\ref{sec:object},
\begin{equation}
\Acc=\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_i \mathrm{Acc}_i,\qquad
\BCR=\tfrac{1}{|B|}\sum_{b\in B}\Phi(\hat Y_b;\mathcal{C}_b),\qquad
\Gapq=\Acc-\BCR,
\label{eq:accbcr}
\end{equation}
and $\mathrm{CSR}_b=\#\{\text{satisfied }c\in\mathcal{C}_b\}/\#\{\text{applicable }c\in\mathcal{C}_b\}$ (with $\mathrm{CSR}_b=1$ if $\mathcal{C}_b=\emptyset$). BCR is the fraction of bundles with $\mathrm{CSR}_b=1$. Acc and BCR have different domains (queries vs.\ bundles) and different predicates (pointwise gold match vs.\ joint constraint satisfaction); $\Gapq$ is therefore not a calibrated residual of Acc.

We also report strict ConsAcc, a predeclared PConsAcc grid, family violation rates, abstention/malformed rates for LLM readers, and world-level bootstrap 95\% CIs (worlds are the primary units).
