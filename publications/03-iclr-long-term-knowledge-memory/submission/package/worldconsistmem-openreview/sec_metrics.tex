\section{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}

Metrics are reported jointly. We do not collapse evaluation into a single headline score. Strict BCR remains the primary consistency rate; CSR and partial ConsAcc are complementary diagnostics, especially when BCR floors.

\subsection{Per-query accuracy}

For query $q_i$ with gold $y_i^*$ and prediction $\hat y_i$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Acc}_i = \mathbf{1}[\hat y_i \equiv y_i^*]
\label{eq:acci}
\end{equation}
under structured equality (IDs, times, relation slots). Mean query accuracy $\Acc$ averages $\mathrm{Acc}_i$ over all scored queries. We also report accuracy by query family.

\subsection{Bundle Consistency Rate (BCR)}

Let $B$ be the set of bundles. With $\Phi$ as in Section~\ref{sec:object},
\begin{equation}
\BCR
=
\frac{1}{|B|}
\sum_{b\in B}
\mathbf{1}\bigl[\Phi(\hat Y_b;\mathcal{C}_b)=1\bigr].
\label{eq:bcr}
\end{equation}
BCR is \textbf{strict}: a single violated applicable constraint yields $\Phi=0$ for that bundle. Under weak readers, BCR can floor at zero even when systems differ in how many constraints they satisfy.

\subsection{Constraint Satisfaction Rate (CSR)}

For bundle $b$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CSR}_b
=
\frac{\#\{\text{satisfied constraints in }b\}}
{\#\{\text{applicable constraints in }b\}},
\label{eq:csrb}
\end{equation}
with $\mathrm{CSR}_b=1$ when $\mathcal{C}_b=\emptyset$. Equivalently, $\BCR$ is the fraction of bundles with $\mathrm{CSR}_b=1$. We report mean and median CSR, CSR distributions, CSR by constraint family, and CSR by difficulty/tier.

We also report the mean/median number of violated constraints and normalized violations $1-\mathrm{CSR}_b$ when $|\mathcal{C}_b|>0$. CSR does \textbf{not} replace BCR. It provides resolution when almost no bundle is fully consistent.

\subsection{Consistency-aware accuracy}

\textbf{Strict ConsAcc.} Fraction of bundles that are fully correct ($\mathrm{Acc}_i=1$ for all $i\in Q_b$) \textbf{and} $\Phi=1$.

\textbf{Relaxed / partial ConsAcc.} (i) Relaxed ConsAcc: mean query Acc on the bundle $\ge \tau$ and $\Phi=1$ (default $\tau=0.8$). (ii) Partial ConsAcc grid $\mathrm{PConsAcc}_{\tau,\tau_c}$: fraction of bundles with mean query Acc $\ge \tau$ and $\mathrm{CSR}_b\ge \tau_c$, for predeclared $\tau\in\{0.5,0.8\}$ and $\tau_c\in\{0.8,0.9\}$. We report the \textbf{full grid}; we do not cherry-pick a favorable cell as a primary claim.

\subsection{Accuracy--consistency gaps}

Primary gap:
\begin{equation}
\Gapq = \Acc - \BCR.
\label{eq:gapq}
\end{equation}
Secondary gap:
\begin{equation}
\Gapb = \mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{bundle}} - \mathrm{ConsAcc}_{\mathrm{strict}},
\label{eq:gapb}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{bundle}}$ is the fraction of bundles with all answers individually correct. Large positive $\Gapq$ indicates high per-query Acc relative to strict joint consistency.

\subsection{Family diagnostics}

We report temporal and provenance violation rates; constraint-family violation rates (fraction of bundles with $\ge 1$ failure in each family); high-accuracy inconsistent rate (mean Acc $\ge 0.8$ and $\Phi=0$); and abstention/malformed rates for LLM readers.

\subsection{Statistical protocol}

Primary units for confidence intervals are \textbf{worlds}, not queries. We report world-level bootstrap 95\% CIs for Acc, BCR, ConsAcc, and CSR where applicable. Queries within a bundle are not treated as independent samples.
