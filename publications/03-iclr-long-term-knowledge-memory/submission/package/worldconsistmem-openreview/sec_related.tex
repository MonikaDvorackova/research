\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

We situate WorldConsistMem against long-term memory evaluation~\citep{wu2025longMemEval,wu2026longMemEvalV2,hu2025memoryAgentBench,wang2026evoMemBench,hu2026everMemBench}, evolving-knowledge and lifecycle benchmarks~\citep{xie2026dynamicMem,liu2026worldMemArena}, conflict/temporal validity work~\citep{tao2026memConflict}, and adjacent cross-query logical consistency~\citep{salla2026crossQueryContradictions,chaudhry2026logicVault}. Existing LTM benchmarks primarily emphasize per-query or per-instance Acc (or closely related scores) under long histories, updates, conflict, or lifecycle protocols. Adjacent logical-consistency work already studies Acc--global-coherence separation for multi-query reasoning/case files, but not for persistent memory systems over evolving multi-entity gold worlds with world-history-derived constraints.

The residual is therefore not ``memory over time'' alone, nor ``consistency'' alone, but their intersection under machine-checkable world-history constraints: dependent query bundles, one shared gold history, BCR, and CSR. A detailed benchmark-by-benchmark discussion and comparison table appear in Appendix~\ref{app:related} (Table~\ref{tab:closest}).
