# Open questions for coauthors

Concrete questions that block finalizing Monika’s text. Repository does **not** already answer these.

## System and method

1. What **exact system** is being integrated (name, repo URL, existing product/research codebase)?
2. What is the proposed method **beyond** the five-layer taxonomy (algorithmic claim, learning objective, retrieval/write policy)?
3. Which of working / session / long-term / graph / archive are **implemented** today vs planned?
4. What is **learned** versus **rule-based** (training? prompts only? symbolic lifecycle?)?
5. What does **knowledge lifecycle** mean operationally (operators, triggers, invariants)?

## Benchmark and data

6. What benchmark **already exists** (path, paper, or internal prototype)?
7. What datasets or **world generators** are available, and who owns them?
8. How are gold answers defined for historical / conflicting / multi-hop / temporal tasks?
9. What is the **primary outcome metric** (and secondary metrics)?
10. Is LLM-as-judge used for any primary score?

## Experiments

11. What is the **empirical hypothesis** (directional, with approximate effect expectation if any)?
12. Which **baselines** will actually run (names + fair protocol)?
13. Which **ablations** are committed for the submission?
14. What models / hardware / approximate runtime budget?
15. Who runs experiments, and what is the freeze date for results tables?

## Authorship and venue

16. What is the **target ICLR submission year and deadline**?
17. Who are the **authors** (order, affiliations, corresponding author)?
18. Confirm section ownership against [`author-responsibilities.md`](author-responsibilities.md) — any changes?
19. Is there a written collaboration agreement / contribution checklist?

## Tooling

20. What is the **authoritative Overleaf project** (URL, owner, compile target)?
21. Who owns the **Zotero collection** and BibTeX export for this paper?
22. Preferred citation-key convention relative to Better BibTeX / this lab’s `authorYearShortTitle`?
23. Where should Monika’s mid-August drafts land first: **this repo**, Overleaf, or both with a sync rule?

## Scope hygiene

24. Should closed exploratory work (`cr-gap-pilot`, topic-search packages) be cited, ignored, or explicitly excluded from Related Work?
25. Is “long-term knowledge consistency” the locked paper keyword, or is another title preferred?
