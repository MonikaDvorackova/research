## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this workspace |
|---|---|
| Topic | Atomic unit of research under `topics/<slug>/` |
| Charter | Topic constitution: purpose, scope, non-goals (`charter.md`) |
| Specification | Versioned normative document under `specifications/` |
| Publication | Venue-shaped narrative under `publications/` derived from topics |
| Spine | Claim→section map and venue plan inside a publication draft |
| Catalog | Machine-readable index in `catalog/*.yaml` |
| Inbox | Untriaged capture under `inbox/` |
| Archive | Frozen topics under `archive/` |
