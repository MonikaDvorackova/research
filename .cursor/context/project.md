# Project Context

## Research Programme

This repository studies the preservation and reconstruction of decision
knowledge in legally relevant AI systems.

## Central Problem

Existing AI documentation, lineage, monitoring, provenance, audit, and
explainability approaches often preserve only fragments of the information
needed to reconstruct a past AI-supported decision.

The research investigates what information must be preserved so that a later
reviewer can reconstruct:

- the relevant system state,
- the decision process,
- the evidence used,
- the applicable rules and constraints,
- the human and organisational context,
- the resulting decision,
- and its consequences over time.
