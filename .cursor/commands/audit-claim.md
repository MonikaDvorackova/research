# Audit Research Claim

Audit the claim specified by the user.

Determine:

1. the exact wording,
2. the claim type,
3. the scope,
4. the supporting evidence,
5. counter-evidence or alternative interpretations,
6. hidden assumptions,
7. whether the evidence supports the strength of the wording,
8. whether the claim should be retained, narrowed, split, or rejected.

Do not improve the wording before checking evidentiary support.

Finish with exactly one primary status:

- supported,
- provisionally supported,
- underdetermined,
- overstated,
- contradicted,
- not yet testable.

Choose the status that best describes the current wording of the claim.

Do not combine multiple primary statuses. Secondary concerns may be noted
separately, but the final audit status must contain exactly one value.
