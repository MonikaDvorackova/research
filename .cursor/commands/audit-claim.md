# Audit Research Claim

Audit the claim specified by the user.

Determine:

1. the exact wording,
2. the claim type,
3. the scope,
4. the supporting evidence,
5. counter-evidence or alternative interpretations,
6. hidden assumptions,
7. whether the evidence supports the strength of the wording,
8. whether the claim should be retained, narrowed, split, or rejected.

Do not improve the wording before checking evidentiary support.

Finish with one status:

- supported,
- provisionally supported,
- underdetermined,
- overstated,
- contradicted,
- not yet testable.
